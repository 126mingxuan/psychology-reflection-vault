# First-Contact Onboarding Questionnaire

## Purpose

Use this only when a user first creates a private working vault. The goal is to collect minimal setup context, not to diagnose the user.

Ask one question at a time. The user may skip any question.

## Questions

1. What made you want to start this recurring reflection space now?
2. What has felt most emotionally important or difficult recently?
3. Which area matters most right now: emotion, relationships, family, study or work, self-worth, habits, or life direction?
4. When things become difficult, what do you usually notice first: thoughts, emotions, body sensations, avoidance, conflict, or numbness?
5. What style would be most useful: more emotional support, deeper analysis, practical steps, or a balance?
6. Are there topics you do not want the assistant to bring up unless you mention them first?
7. What would make this process feel genuinely useful after several sessions?

## What To Save

Save only what is useful for continuity:

- language and communication preferences;
- broad current goals;
- preferred session style;
- privacy boundaries;
- initial areas of concern;
- one first-session opening direction.

Do not pressure the user for identity details, medical history, financial details, private messages, or other sensitive data.
