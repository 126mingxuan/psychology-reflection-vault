# Adaptive Strategy Routing

## Goal

Use the strategy that best fits the user's current material. Do not force every session into one theory.

## Routing Table

| Signal | Primary strategy | Useful response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated relationship conflict, shame, attachment, self-worth tension | Psychodynamic or psychoanalytic | Explore repeated patterns, defenses, fears, and unmet needs. |
| Rumination, avoidance, anxiety loop, procrastination, action difficulty | CBT-style tools | Identify automatic thoughts, small behaviors, and next actions. |
| Family role pressure, loyalty conflict, boundaries, inherited expectations | Family systems | Map roles, boundaries, alliances, and repeated family scripts. |
| Body tension, emotional flooding, numbness, over-analysis | Mindfulness-based reflection | Ground attention, name sensations, slow the session pace. |
| Meaning, life direction, loneliness, freedom, responsibility | Existential reflection | Explore values, choices, limits, and responsibility without rushing advice. |
| Need for acceptance, shame reduction, emotional holding | Humanistic support | Offer warmth, accurate reflection, and nonjudgmental presence. |
| Self-harm intent, harm-to-others intent, severe loss of control, immediate danger | Safety-first | Stop ordinary reflection and prioritize real-world safety. |

## Selection Rules

- Start with the user's own language and stated concern.
- Use the lightest strategy that can help.
- Combine strategies when useful, but keep the response simple.
- Name hypotheses as provisional.
- If risk appears, safety overrides all other strategies.

## Recording The Strategy

At the end of the session note, include:

- primary strategy used;
- secondary strategy if any;
- why this strategy fit the material;
- whether to continue or shift strategy next time.

## Examples

```text
Primary strategy used: CBT-style tools
Reason: The session focused on avoidance and action difficulty.
Next strategy: Combine CBT with psychodynamic reflection if shame or self-worth themes repeat.
```

```text
Primary strategy used: Psychodynamic reflection
Reason: The material centered on repeated relationship patterns and fear of rejection.
Next strategy: Continue gently; avoid premature conclusions until more evidence appears.
```
