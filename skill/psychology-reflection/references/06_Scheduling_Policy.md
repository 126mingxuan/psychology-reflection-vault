# Adaptive Scheduling Policy

## Default

- Frequency: once per week.
- Duration: 45 to 60 minutes.
- Adjust timing based on emotional intensity, unfinished material, stability, processing load, and risk signals.

## Recommended Timing

- 2 to 3 days: high intensity or unfinished material.
- 4 to 5 days: important material should continue soon but is not acute.
- 7 days: stable and normal weekly rhythm fits.
- 10 to 14 days: the user needs more time to digest or observe.
